YouTube Automation

Privacy policy

Effective: 12 November 2026

YouTube Automation is a private desktop application operated by Mukul for the owner's authorized YouTube channels. This policy describes the application's current data handling and this public informational website. Contact: bententhousan@gmail.com.

Information the application accesses

With the owner's permission, the application uses Google OAuth and YouTube APIs to access:

  • Channel identity, including the channel name, handle, and immutable channel ID, to verify and route each connection.
  • Channel and video information, including titles, publication dates, duration, and privacy status.
  • Available performance measurements, such as views, watch time, viewing duration and percentage, subscriber changes, likes, comments counts, and shares.
  • Authorization tokens used to maintain access without signing in for every job.

When publishing is enabled for an authorized channel, the application sends approved video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, privacy settings, and scheduling instructions to YouTube. The requested OAuth permissions depend on whether the connection is for read-only analysis or publishing. This application does not ask for or store the owner's Google password.

Why the information is used

Information is used to verify the correct channel, collect performance reports, avoid repeated topics, research and produce content, operate publishing jobs, and recover safely from interrupted work. Channel information is not sold or used by the operator for advertising profiles.

The application uses the YouTube API Services. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This commitment is not a statement that Google has verified or endorsed the application.

Services involved in processing

Google and YouTube. Authorization, channel verification, analytics retrieval, and authorized publishing use Google and YouTube services. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

OpenAI. Research and scripting use the owner's signed-in Codex service. Requests may include recent episode titles, formats, dates, script openings, selected aggregate channel measurements, and a small sample of video titles and performance measurements. These inputs help plan the owner's channel content. Sanitization excludes OAuth credentials and tokens; it does not make channel measurements anonymous. OAuth client files, access tokens, refresh tokens, and passwords are not included in these model prompts.

OpenAI processes these requests under its applicable terms, the owner's account settings, and the OpenAI Privacy Policy. This application cannot promise a provider retention period or training setting. The owner should review those controls before enabling the workflow.

Additional creator analytics services are not part of the current connected workflow. If the owner enables another integration, its access and data practices must be reviewed and this policy updated where needed.

Local storage and protection

Each channel's saved credentials and resumable upload-session records are encrypted on the operator's Windows computer using current-user Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Their storage directories use restricted Windows access rules for the current user and the operating system. These controls depend on the security of that Windows account and computer.

Operational records are separate from that encrypted credential store. Channel connection summaries, analytics snapshots, job history, research, scripts, review reports, and rendered media are stored as local files or a local database and are not all encrypted by this application. The original downloaded OAuth client configuration is also a separate, operator-managed local file. The owner is responsible for device access, backups, and protecting these files.

No security measure guarantees complete protection. The public website contains no credentials, analytics records, or private channel files.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local credentials and operational records remain on the operator's computer until the owner removes them. There is currently no automatic time-based deletion policy. Authorization may expire or be revoked earlier, so unattended access is not guaranteed indefinitely.

The owner can stop the local worker, revoke the application's access through Google Account connections, and remove the corresponding local encrypted credentials, analytics files, job records, and production files. Removing the local credentials alone does not revoke access at Google; revocation and local cleanup are separate actions. Questions or help with local cleanup can be directed to the contact address above.

Revoking access or removing local data does not delete content already uploaded to YouTube. The application does not automatically delete videos. The owner manages published content separately in YouTube.

This informational website

This site has no sign-in, contact form, analytics script, advertising script, or application-set tracking cookie. Following an external link takes the visitor to that service's own website. Sending email provides the information the sender chooses to include to the operator and the email service.

The website's hosting provider may receive technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, requested page, and access time, under its own practices. Publishing this static site does not connect a visitor's Google or YouTube account to the desktop application.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application's data handling changes. The effective date appears above. For a privacy question or deletion assistance, contact bententhousan@gmail.com.
