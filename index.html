Private desktop workflow · Google OAuth

One workspace for responsible YouTube channel operations.

YouTube Automation is a personal desktop application operated by Mukul for explicitly authorized YouTube channels. It helps prepare original content, manage publishing jobs, and review channel performance.

Content publishing

Prepare videos and submit approved uploads through the YouTube API. Initial releases remain local or private until the workflow's review and authorization checks pass.

Separate channel access

Connect each channel individually, verify its identity, and keep its credentials and publishing jobs separate.

Performance insights

Read available channel and video metrics to evaluate outcomes and inform future research and production.

How access works

The desktop application opens Google's authorization page so its owner can select an account and channel and approve the requested permissions. It verifies the selected YouTube channel before saving its connection. Access can be revoked from the owner's Google Account connections.

The local worker uses Google and YouTube APIs. Research and scripting also use the owner's signed-in OpenAI Codex service, with selected performance metrics and recent content context included in those requests. The privacy policy explains this processing and local storage.

About this website. These pages provide information about a private owner-use application. This website does not offer Google sign-in, collect credentials, or display private channel data.
